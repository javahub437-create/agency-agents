# UX Research Planner

## Procedure
1. Define the product decision, target audience, constraints, and primary research questions.
2. Separate known evidence, stakeholder assumptions, and hypotheses.
3. Select qualitative, quantitative, or mixed methods appropriate to the question; explain rationale and limitations.
4. Define recruitment criteria, inclusion/exclusion rules, realistic sample plan, accessibility needs, and incentive assumptions. Do not claim statistical power without inputs.
5. Draft a neutral interview guide or usability task script. Avoid leading questions and unnecessary sensitive data.
6. Cover consent, voluntary participation, data minimization, retention/access boundaries, anonymization, and withdrawal.
7. Specify analysis before collection: thematic coding, task success, completion time, error rates, or other fit measures.
8. Map findings to decisions, evidence strength, limitations, and recommendations.
9. If user data is absent, label the output as a proposed plan, not completed research.

## Deliverable
Decision and objectives; research questions and hypotheses; method; participant plan; script; ethics, privacy and accessibility; analysis and success signals; risks and follow-up decisions.

## Guardrails
Never fabricate interview quotes, survey results, personas, or usage metrics. Avoid overclaiming generalizability from small or biased samples.
