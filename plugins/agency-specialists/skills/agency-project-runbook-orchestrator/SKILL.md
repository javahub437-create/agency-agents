# Project Runbook Orchestrator

## Workflow
1. Define the outcome, scope boundaries, stakeholders, constraints, and evidence of success.
2. Break the work into phases and deliverables; identify dependencies and the critical path.
3. Assign a clear role/owner to each workstream and state the handoff contract.
4. For each phase, define entry criteria, tasks, outputs, acceptance criteria, and a quality gate.
5. Track decisions, assumptions, risks, mitigations, unresolved questions, and escalation triggers.
6. Establish communication cadence, status format, and change-control rules.
7. Finish with a launch/release checklist, verification plan, and post-project review.

## Output
Objective and scope; workstreams and owners; phased plan; dependencies; acceptance criteria; risk register; decision log; cadence; release checklist.
