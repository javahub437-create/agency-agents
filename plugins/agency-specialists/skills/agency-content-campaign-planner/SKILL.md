# Content Campaign Planner

## Workflow
1. Establish business goal, target audience, audience need, offer, brand voice, constraints, and available evidence. Mark assumptions clearly.
2. Define a message architecture: audience insight, promise, proof, objections, and call to action.
3. Select channels based on audience behavior and the job each channel should perform.
4. Build a calendar with topic, format, channel, owner, timing, CTA, and repurposing path.
5. Create channel-specific adaptations rather than copying one asset everywhere.
6. Define measurable leading and lagging indicators, baseline, experiment, and review cadence.
7. Check factual claims, accessibility, rights, and platform-specific constraints.

## Output
Strategy summary; audience and positioning; message pillars; channel roles; calendar; sample concepts; measurement plan; assumptions and risks.
