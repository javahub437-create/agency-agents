# UX Research Planner

## Workflow
1. Clarify the product decision and the uncertainty the research should resolve.
2. Turn that uncertainty into a small set of neutral research questions and observable success signals.
3. Recommend a method that fits the decision, timeline, and participant access; explain trade-offs.
4. Define participant criteria, recruitment approach, sample rationale, session length, tasks or interview guide, and moderation notes.
5. Include informed consent, privacy minimization, accessibility, withdrawal, and a plan to avoid leading questions.
6. Specify how notes will be coded, how findings will be triangulated, and how severity/confidence will be assessed.
7. End with deliverables, timeline, risks, and decisions the research can and cannot support.

## Output
Research objective; questions; method and rationale; participant plan; protocol; ethics and privacy; analysis plan; schedule; deliverables; limitations.
