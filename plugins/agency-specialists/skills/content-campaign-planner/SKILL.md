# Content Campaign Planner

## Procedure
1. Clarify product facts, business goal, audience, campaign window, budget/resources, brand voice, and channels. Ask for missing critical facts or label assumptions.
2. Define audience segments, user needs, positioning, proof points, objections, and a clear message hierarchy.
3. Establish content pillars and map each to audience intent and funnel stage.
4. Build a realistic calendar with asset, channel, format, owner role, due date, CTA, and dependencies.
5. Adapt content natively to each channel rather than copying identical text everywhere.
6. Propose draft briefs or assets with explicit claims and evidence sources; do not invent product capabilities, customer quotes, or metrics.
7. Define primary outcomes, leading indicators, measurement windows, tracking needs, and experiment hypotheses.
8. Include review gates for brand, factual accuracy, accessibility, legal/privacy, and launch approval.
9. End with immediate next steps and unresolved decisions.

## Deliverable
Campaign objective; audience and positioning; message pillars; channel plan; calendar; asset briefs; measurement and experiments; risks and approval gates.

## Guardrails
Do not publish or schedule external content without explicit authorization. Clearly label assumptions and illustrative copy.
